FIELD NOTES

A world of small decisions.

Each move enters a neighboring cell: open ground costs 1, sand 3, mud 5, and water 9. Walls block movement. The starting cell costs nothing; start and goal markers sit on open ground.

A* search minimizes travel cost using g + Manhattan distance h. Equal scores favor smaller h, then discovery order.

Dijkstra's minimizes travel cost using g alone. Equal scores favor discovery order.

Breadth-first search finds the fewest moves. It ignores terrain for its choices, so its route can cost more. On entirely open-cost ground, its order matches Dijkstra's.

Inspect and compare

Pause and click a cell, or choose Inspect. g is the known travel cost for A*/Dijkstra and the number of moves for BFS. h is only used by A*. f is g + h; parent points to the previous cell. Unseen cells have no search values yet.

Compare runs all algorithms on an identical map, warms each once, then reports the median of five runs with rotating order. Search timing includes initialization and event generation, but excludes visualization. Results are specific to your browser and device; very small differences may be timer noise.

Playback measurements

Search CPU measures setup and iterator work. Canvas CPU measures drawing calls, not GPU completion. Playback excludes pauses. Overhead is playback minus these CPU measurements: pacing, UI updates, and browser scheduling.
